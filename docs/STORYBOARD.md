# Ten-frame artwork

The image in `public/story/fictional-journey-10-steps.webp` is a fictional photographic storyboard made with the built-in image generator on 17 October 2026. It is not clinical evidence and is not connected to the sample timeline measurements.

The same imagined adult appears across ten panels. The intended visual direction was consistent identity, camera framing, light and skin texture, with gradual changes in expression and depigmentation. The final image includes a visible disclosure that it was generated with AI and does not show treatment results or promise recovery.

Prompt direction: Create a photographic storyboard in a five-column, two-row grid. Use the same fictional 28-year-old woman with medium-brown skin, dark curly hair and a mint top in every frame. Keep the face and both hands visible under consistent soft daylight. Start with a quietly worried expression and visible vitiligo-like patches. Across ten imaginary stages, reduce the patches gradually in matching locations and move the expression towards a natural smile. Use realistic skin texture and subtle mint and coral borders. Number the frames 01 through 10. Include the exact footer: “AI-generated fictional journey. Not a treatment result or a promise of recovery.” No products, prescriptions, statistics or claims of app effectiveness.
