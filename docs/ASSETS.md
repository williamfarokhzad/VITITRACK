# Doctor profile artwork

Project asset: `public/profiles/demo-doctor.webp`.

Created with the built-in image generation tool, then resized and encoded as a 512-pixel WebP for the app. The person is fictional and is not a clinical collaborator or an endorsement. The same illustration is used by the doctor demo profile and the patient's care-message shortcut; live messages are still separately authenticated.

Prompt: A professional editorial head-and-shoulders portrait of a fictional female dermatologist, about 40, with warm medium skin, short dark curly hair, a kind relaxed expression, a white coat over muted teal scrubs and a subtle stethoscope. Natural skin texture and proportions, soft daylight, a pale mint clinic background, face centered with padding for circular cropping. No text, badge, logo, watermark, other people or identifiable public figure.

# Illustrated hand timeline

Assets: `public/timeline/illustrative-hand-step-1.webp` through `illustrative-hand-step-6.webp`, plus the contact sheet `illustrative-hand-sequence.webp`.

Created with the built-in image generation tool as a six-panel fictional sequence, then cropped into six 512-pixel WebP frames. The same right hand, mint background, pose and lighting are retained while the light patches become smaller. These are imagined illustrations, not photographs from a patient, measured changes, treatment evidence or a recovery prediction. The sequence is separate from profile records and chart values.

Prompt brief: photorealistic six-panel contact sheet, three columns by two rows; same fictional medium-brown right hand with identical camera framing, position and soft daylight in every panel; mint background; imagined gradual reduction of light patches with some residual patches at the end; no labels, text, treatment objects or promises of complete recovery.
