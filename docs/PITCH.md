# One-minute founder pitch

I’ve lived with vitiligo myself. Sometimes, I looked at my skin and wondered: is it getting better, or is it just the light?

That’s why I started WIFA VitiTrack.

Our mission is to make it easier for people with vitiligo to follow changes in their skin.

The idea is simple: take a photo, record your care, and keep everything in one place.

Our vision is to help patients and doctors understand the journey together, with clearer information.

Our first goal is to build an easy-to-use app that helps patients take more consistent photos and prepare a useful report for their doctor.

For me, this is personal. I want to turn my own experience into something useful for others.
